## 2. Policy on Missing Information (No Fake Fabrication)
- **Photos:** Where individual staff or management portraits were not provided with high-resolution digital cutouts in the PDF, a **strict professional placeholder** with initials and "Photo Coming Soon" is rendered. No fake AI faces are generated.
- **SuperAdmin Photo Upload:** SuperAdmin can upload real photographs directly via the CMS at any time.
- **Demo Data:** Synthetic demo data is only used for student portal demonstration (mock attendance records, mock marks) and is clearly isolated from the verified institutional facts.
